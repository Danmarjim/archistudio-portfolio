The nursery of **Casa Archi & Colori** was published in **HOME no. 36, April 2026**, in the Spazio 0-12 section.

In a fully renovated Milanese apartment by MP_Archistudio, the youngest child's bedroom becomes an evolving space that reimagines the sleeping area as a dynamic, cosmopolitan microcosm.

A warm sunny yellow warms the nursery walls: the lace-like detail repeated across all surfaces makes the room feel intimate and welcoming, with a playful, tailored touch that is echoed — in a reversed colour-block game — on the wardrobe doors as well. The same yellow reappears in the children's bathroom, where the floor tiles evoke the effect of brushstrokes painted by hand, and the furnishings follow the rule of two: two mirrors, two sinks, two spotlights, two stools — everything doubled, in a play of perfect symmetry that turns the morning routine into a moment of pure design.
