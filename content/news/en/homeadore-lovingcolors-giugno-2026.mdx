**Homeadore**, an international architecture and interior design blog, published *"Lovingcolors Opens a 1960s Apartment by Martina Pozzi"* in June 2026, about the full renovation of an 80 m² apartment in Bergamo.

The article highlights the choices that transformed a typical 1960s plan:

- kitchen, dining and living room combined into a single open day area, removing the long corridor;
- the large iron-and-glass sliding door that closes off the kitchen without breaking visual continuity;
- at the entrance, a fitted wall that uses the wall's varying depths: shoe cabinet, sideboard, coat storage and a concealed 7 m² home office;
- colour used with precision, giving each room a clear function and its own character.
