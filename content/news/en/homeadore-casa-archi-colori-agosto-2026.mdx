In August 2026 **Homeadore** published *"Casa ARCHI & COLORI: A Playful Milan Apartment"*, about the full renovation of a 155 m² apartment in Milan.

The article covers:

- the visual and spatial connection between kitchen, dining and living room;
- bespoke joinery treated as part of the architecture: groove handles, curved shelves, the flush arched door to the night area;
- the green built-in bookcase in the living room, with a bench under the window, and the cabinet that hides the piano and a complete home office;
- the sequence of green, yellow, blue and pastel rooms, separated by a white corridor that acts as a pause.

The same project was also published in **HOME no. 36** (April 2026), with the nursery.
