ArchiAdvice is my online architecture consultation: a one-hour video call to clear up your doubts about your home, from colours to materials, from furniture to layout. It's designed for anyone with a specific question that doesn't need a full project, or who wants an architect's opinion before spending money. It costs €100, all inclusive. You book online in a few minutes and join from home, wherever you are, in Italian, Spanish or English.

## What you can solve in an hour

You can get a long way in 60 minutes if the question is clear. The most common requests:

- **Colours and materials.** Which colour for the walls, how to match floors, tiles and furniture, how to get the look you have in mind without buying the wrong things.
- **Layout and furniture.** How to arrange furniture, what to keep and what to replace, how to make the most of an awkward corner or a room that doesn't work.
- **Where to buy.** Advice on types of furniture, finishes and suppliers that suit your budget.
- **A second opinion.** Already have a design or a quote and something doesn't feel right? We look at it together.
- **Renting.** You can't do building work but want to feel at home: colour, light, textiles and furniture do far more than you'd think.
- **Before deciding on a renovation.** Working out whether a restyling is enough or whether services and walls need to change.

## How it works

1. **Book your slot** on Calendly, choosing day and time, and fill in a short questionnaire: the room or question to tackle, who lives with you, the atmosphere and style you want, the colours and materials you love and the ones you can't stand.
2. **Send me photos and a floor plan:** photos of the rooms and, if you have one, a dimensioned floor plan. After booking you receive instructions by email.
3. **Pay for the consultation** by PayPal or bank transfer at least 3 days before the appointment; if payment doesn't arrive, the appointment is cancelled.
4. **We meet on Google Meet** for 60 minutes, from a computer, tablet or smartphone. Since I've already studied your space, the whole hour goes into solutions: practical advice and fresh perspectives on the rooms you want to renovate or refresh.
5. **After the call** you receive the invoice and have what you need to carry on yourself or, if you like, a proposal to continue with a restyling or a full project.

## When an hour isn't enough

An online consultation is perfect for deciding, not for replacing a design project. It's not the right fit if you need to move walls or redo the services, if a building permit is required or if you want someone to supervise the works. In those cases I'll tell you clearly and point you in the right direction: a [restyling without building work](/servicios/restyling-casa) or a [full renovation](/servicios/ristrutturazione-appartamento-bergamo) with design and site supervision.

## Prepare your ArchiAdvice

To get the most out of the hour:

- photograph each room from the corners, in natural light and without zoom;
- note the main measurements or find the floor plan, ideally dimensioned (the land registry one is fine);
- collect 2–3 images of homes or rooms you like;
- note what you want to keep (furniture, objects, artworks) and what you're willing to change;
- think about a rough budget: it changes the possible solutions a lot;
- write down the most important question, the one you absolutely want answered.
