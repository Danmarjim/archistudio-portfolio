Home restyling is a way to change how your spaces look and work **without moving walls or redoing the services**: the tools are colour, light, furniture layout and a few pieces designed to measure. It's the right choice when the home is sound but no longer feels like you, when you're renting, or when building work has just finished and everything else is missing. Colour is at the heart of my work, and in a restyling it's the tool that makes **the biggest difference for the smallest investment**.

## Restyling or renovation?

The difference lies in what gets touched.

| Restyling | Renovation |
|---|---|
| Paint, wallpaper, wall decoration | Demolishing and building partition walls |
| Decorative lighting and lamps | Replacing electrical, plumbing and heating systems |
| Furniture layout and selection | Replacing windows |
| Bespoke pieces: bookcases, wardrobes, benches, headboards | A new layout |
| Textiles, curtains, rugs | Building permits and land registry updates |
| Floors and wall finishes laid over existing ones [DA CONFERMARE: do you treat these as restyling?] | |

If the home needs work on walls and services, a [full renovation](/servicios/ristrutturazione-appartamento-bergamo) is the right path. The two often meet: a well-designed restyling can postpone or scale down a renovation.

## Who it's for

**If your home no longer feels like you.** Tastes change, families grow, people work from home: the spaces stay the same but stop working. A restyling brings them back in line with how you live today.

**If you're renting.** You can't do building work, but you can do a lot: colour, light, textiles, furniture and **reversible solutions you take with you to your next home**. The goal is to feel at home even when the home isn't yours.

**If you've just finished building work.** The renovation is done, the walls are white and furniture, colour and details are missing. This is when a new home risks staying anonymous.

**For shops and offices.** The same tools work for a workspace that needs to tell clients and colleagues who you are.

## How to refresh your home without renovating

- **Colour.** A wall, a ceiling, a perspective backdrop or a painted semicircle can change how a room feels more than any piece of furniture.
- **Light.** Decorative light points, floor and table lamps, the right colour temperature for each room.
- **Furniture layout.** Move, remove, highlight: often the right furniture is already there, just in the wrong place.
- **Bespoke pieces.** Bookcases, wardrobes, benches and headboards designed to use every centimetre and give the space character.
- **Textiles and rugs.** Curtains, upholstery, cushions and rugs add warmth and finish the room. The options include my own [Sevilla rug collection](/tappeti), which can be made to measure and in the project's colours.

## A real project: CASA PEONIA Restyling

A 130 m² home in Camparada, in Brianza, a 2026 project. The client wanted a warmer, more welcoming and modern home that would showcase the designer furniture and artworks they cared about.

- The living area walls went from electric blue to a light ivory that brightens the whole living room.
- A new light oak herringbone floor also covers the stairs, linking the two floors.
- The entrance wall, in a near-black brown, wraps into the living room and frames the existing bookcase and the black travertine fireplace.
- Against this neutral backdrop, the golden yellow of the velvet sofa and painted semicircles on the wall frame a Sardinian rug and a Tuscan ceramic sun.
- The plum-coloured kitchen, with a peony fresco by a local artist, stayed as it was: the bathroom wallpaper picks up its violet and yellow tones.

## Even a single room: kitchen restyling

A restyling doesn't have to cover the whole home. The kitchen is often where a focused intervention changes daily life the most.

- **[PARIGINA Kitchen](/proyectos/cucina-parigina), Monza, 8.2 m².** A 1980s kitchen transformed with a "swimming pool" effect: the floor tiles run up the walls to half height, and a wrought-iron bistro table recalls the streets of Paris.
- **[MITE Kitchen](/proyectos/cucina-mite), Milan, 11 m².** Bespoke full-height units that hide the oven and fridge, polyurethane resin over the existing floor to avoid loading the slab, mirrored surfaces and mink lacquered doors to double the light.
