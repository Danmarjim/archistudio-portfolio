Thinking of buying a home?

## Right for you if:

- You need an architect to review all documents to make sure everything is in order (habitability, land registry...)
- You want a site visit with an architect and/or a shared assessment of the information to evaluate potential issues, discover the property's potential and how to make the most of every square metre
- You want a rough idea of how much it would cost to renovate
- You want to make an informed purchase
