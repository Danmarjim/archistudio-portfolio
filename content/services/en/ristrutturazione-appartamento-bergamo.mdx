Need to renovate?

## A turnkey service designed to take every worry off your shoulders

- Geometric survey and bespoke project tailored to your needs
- Building permits, material selection and quote requests
- Contractor management, financial monitoring and schedule
- Artistic direction, construction management and project closure with cadastral update

## How do we work?

Here are the steps of the 360° service

### 1. Survey

Geometric and photographic survey. We measure every corner of your space and digitise it into 2D drawings that form the basis for every subsequent step. If required, we will access official records to verify the compliance of the current state with the legal state.

### 2. Architectural & Interior Design Project

- **Preliminary design**: Development of a concept layout with furnished floor plans where your dreams begin to take shape.
- **Definitive design**: Sharing of the LOOKS & FEELS board: graphic research of concrete references and emotional inspirations to define the style of the project. Presentation of the MATERIALS MOODBOARD to define colours and materials that follow the project's style. Development of the winning proposal with all graphic documents needed to understand the spaces: floor plans, furnished elevations and 3D renderings, indicating the chosen furniture models and interior finishes.
- **Executive design**: Development of detailed drawings and executive project graphics — demolitions, construction and systems — for requesting quotes and preparing the site dossier. Preparation of the catalogue furniture shopping list. Design of any bespoke furniture pieces.

### 3. Artistic Direction

- Bill of quantities for masonry works.
- Research and management of contractors.
- Assistance with material and finish selection, including requesting samples and arranging showroom visits.
- Requesting and coordinating supplier quotes.
- Site visits to check aesthetic execution in line with the finishes specified in the project.

### 4. Site Coordination

- Sharing of the estimated schedule and organisation of construction phases.
- Site inspections, construction meetings and project compliance checks on technical, regulatory and administrative aspects.
- Accounting review and settlement of measured works — Progress Certificate (SAL).
- Assistance with invoice management for tax deductions and information on available bonuses.

### 5. Planning & Building Permits

Preparation of graphic documents and certified technical reports. Liaison with public authorities and assumption of civil and criminal responsibilities to obtain building permits. Cadastral update procedures and submission of the Final Works Completion notice.
